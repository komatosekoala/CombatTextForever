# Changelog

## 1.2.0

New
- Damage-over-time ticks (Garrote, Rupture, Corruption) are shown as their ability with its icon instead of as white swings

## 1.1.0

New
- Pet hits are shown as their own event with "(Pet)" after the number
- Spells that land after their cast (Fireball, Frostbolt, Aimed Shot) now keep their name
- Notification for short abilities such as Slice and Dice and Blood Fury, with its own toggle and colour
- Option to show or hide ability names on damage numbers
- Colour settings for incoming melee and spell damage
- Mouseover descriptions on every checkbox
- Dropdown lists in the options in place of click-to-cycle buttons

Changed
- White swings, ranged attacks and procs are now shown while in a group
- Incoming melee damage uses Blizzard's red; incoming spell damage is coloured by school
- Reputation and skill-up notifications use the game's chat colours
- Darker default colours for the +Combat, -Combat and honor notifications
- Default text outline is now None, and ability names are off by default
- Removed the white/yellow damage summary shown after each fight

Fixed
- Raptor Strike and other abilities queued onto the next swing showed 0 damage, with the real damage shown as a white swing

## 1.0.0

- Initial release
